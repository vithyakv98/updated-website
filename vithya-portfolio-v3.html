The challenge

20+ organisations in early childhood development — each with their own voice, framing, and priorities. No shared narrative. No coalition identity. Lots of noise, no signal.

What I did

Authored a two-year communications strategy that gave the coalition a shared narrative, consistent messaging, and a framework every member could use. One voice. Many advocates.

The challenge

The Delhi Happiness Curriculum was reaching thousands of students — but the story wasn't reaching government partners, funders, or media. Visibility was the gap.

What I did

500+ pieces of content. Government advocacy with the Delhi Department of Education and Chief Minister's Media Team. BBC coverage that took the project global. Genpact partnership for teacher training at scale.

The context

Chiefs for Change — a US education policy organisation — needed an Annual Report template designed for a sophisticated policy audience. The engagement was short-term.

What I delivered

A designed Annual Report template and a completed section — built to be clean, data-forward, and easy for the internal team to continue and expand independently.
